Distributivity requirement: f(X ∪ Y) = f(X) ∪ f(Y). This holds for all three analyses because: taint of a union = union of taints; nullable of a union = union of nullable; type-states of a union = union of type-states. Distributivity enables path merging at join points in polynomial time via the Reps–Horwitz–Sagiv tabulation algorithm.
